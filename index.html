Our Name

The Meaning of Odyssey

Odyssey — from the Greek — means a journey filled with growth, challenges and discoveries. It symbolises a voyage through harsh waters and storms, a test of strength and resilience.

Much like school life. Every student embarks on a journey of personal growth. We're here to support each step — personal problems, academic struggles, whatever comes up. We lead them to success.

Odyssey 指的是一段充滿成長、挑戰與發現的旅程。這個源自希臘的名字，象徵著穿越險惡水域與風暴的艱難航程。

我們期盼能陪伴學生走過這趟旅程的每一步，無論是個人或學業上的問題，都將竭盡所能提供支援，引領他們邁向成功的里程碑。

Our Aim

What We Do

Learning doesn't stop at the classroom. We know that. At Odyssey, students come first. We lead by example, listen when it matters, and push for change where it's needed.

We're the bridge between students and the administration. Our job: make this year unforgettable.

我們深知，學生時代是成長、學習與共享體驗的重要篇章，其意義遠遠超越了課堂的範疇。

我們將每位學生的興趣放在首位。我們致力於以包容的態度引領、以謙遜的態度傾聽，並作為學生群體與校方管理層之間不可或缺的溝通橋樑。
