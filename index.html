Our Name

The Meaning of Odyssey

Odyssey refers to a journey filled with growth, challenges and discoveries. This word originates from Greek and symbolises a challenging voyage through harsh waters and storms — a true test of strength and resilience.

Much like school life, this is an adventure where every student embarks on a journey of personal growth, discovery, and resilience. We hope to support our students through each step of this voyage, no matter it being personal or academic problems, and lead them to success.

Odyssey 指的是一段充滿成長、挑戰與發現的旅程。這個源自希臘的名字，象徵著穿越險惡水域與風暴的艱難航程，更是對力量與韌性的真正考驗。

我們相信，這恰如我們的校園生活：一場冒險，每位學生都將踏上這段個人成長的旅程。我們期盼能陪伴學生走過這趟旅程的每一步，無論是個人或學業上的問題，都將竭盡所能提供支援，引領他們邁向成功的里程碑。

Our Aim

Our Mission

We understand that as a student, learning is a massive chapter of growth, learning and shared experiences that goes far beyond the classroom.

At Odyssey, we put students' interests first. We are committed to leading by example, listening humbly and doing change where needed, acting as the vital liaison between the student body and the school administration. Our goal is to make this school year unforgettable for every student through a variety of events and initiatives.

我們深知，學生時代是成長、學習與共享體驗的重要篇章，其意義遠遠超越了課堂的範疇。

我們將每位學生的興趣放在首位，以此打造一段成功且難忘的旅程。我們致力於以包容的態度引領、以謙遜的態度傾聽、以開放的態度倡導，並作為學生群體與校方管理層之間不可或缺的溝通橋樑。我們的目標是透過各種精彩紛呈的活動與計畫，為每位學生的本學年創造難忘的回憶。

What We Do

Key Events

Sep – Oct

Easter Appreciation Day

Egg design workshops, scavenger hunts, and Easter-themed casual wear day.

復活節感恩日 — 復活蛋設計工作坊、尋寶活動、復活節便服日。

Oct – Nov

Mental Awareness Week

Promoting mental health through workshops, talks, and wellness activities.

心理健康周 — 透過工作坊、講座及健康活動推廣心理健康。

Nov

Spirit Week

A week of themed casual wear days, house competitions, and school spirit events.

精神周 — 主題便服日、社際比賽及校內精神活動。

Dec

Eco-Friendly Week

Sustainability initiatives, recycling drives, and environmental awareness campaigns.

環保周 — 可持續發展倡议、回收活動及環保意識推廣。

Dec

Christmas Ball

Joint-school Christmas celebration with performances, games, and festive activities.

聖誕舞會 — 聯校聖誕慶祝活動，包括表演、遊戲及節日活動。

Jan – Feb

Bake Sale

Student-baked goods sale, raising funds for SA activities and welfare.

烘焙義賣 — 學生自製烘焙食品售賣，為學生活動及福利籌款。

Mar – Apr

Students' Day

Sports & esports competitions, society booths, talent show, and food stalls.

學生日 — 體育及電競比賽、學會攤位、才藝表演及美食攤位。

Jun – Jul

Senior Sign Off

A farewell celebration for graduating seniors — memories, messages, and appreciation.

畢業生送舊會 — 為畢業生舉行的歡送慶祝活動，留下回憶與祝福。
